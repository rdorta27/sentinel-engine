SentinelHackathon 2026 · 6 / 6

What comes next, and what it needs.

Limits

  • Data: synthetic, Spanish only, three countries. A model wrote the Portuguese cases.
  • Ceiling: the policy lets 16 of 56 simulated cases resolve (Simulation). The system resolves all of them, and no others.
  • Mocks: Gold store, login, advisor, case store and policy values. Each has a production replacement.
  • Numbers: no number here is a production measurement.

Roadmap

  • Customer 360: the balance has no usable as-of date.
  • Charge investigation: it needs real bank data.
  • Feedback dataset: learn from the outcome of each handoff.
  • Spending assistant: it needs balances and history.
  • Policy retrieval: it needs the policy documents of the bank.
  • Handoff routing: by language and specialty.
