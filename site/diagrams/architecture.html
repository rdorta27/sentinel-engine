Orchestrator + router

Real Model labels

A prompted LLM labels the intent. It decides nothing else. A keyword baseline answers a turn when the model fails.

In the demo
GLM 5.3 Flash on Fireworks AI with live calls. The keyword baseline is the per-turn fallback.
In production
The same weights on Azure AI Foundry or Databricks.
